DANDA #077 — VetRecords AI: Autonomous Agents to End Lost Pet Health Records and Enable Seamless Care

AR Akhil Reddy Danda · 6th October, 2026 · 4 min read

Every day, pet owners and veterinarians struggle to access accurate, up-to-date health records for the 197 million dogs and cats in the United States. Vaccination histories, medication lists, allergies, and surgical notes are scattered across fragmented clinic systems, lost in paper files, or simply missing when they're needed most—during emergencies, travel, or changes in vet care. This chaos leads to millions in avoidable costs, unnecessary repeat tests, and even tragic outcomes for pets.

The problem

Pet health data is a disaster. Over 30,000 veterinary clinics and hospitals in the US use a patchwork of outdated practice management systems—almost none of which interoperate. Pet owners move, travel, or change vets, and their records vanish into the ether. Less than 15% of clinics have any API connectivity for record sharing. Shelters and rescues lose track of vaccination and spay/neuter info, so the same animals get re-vaccinated, or worse, go without protection. In emergencies, 911 calls involving pets often mean critical care is delayed while staff call old clinics for faxed records—faxes that often never come.

The economic damage is enormous: over $1.6 billion a year is wasted in unnecessary re-testing and duplicate vaccinations. Pet owners waste hours chasing records, while clinics face liability risk for incomplete or incorrect care. Regulatory bodies demand better animal health oversight, yet there's no national standard or clearinghouse. The problem is worse for low-income owners, multi-pet households, and during disasters when rapid evacuation separates pets from their paperwork entirely.

The idea: VetRecords AI

VetRecords AI is an autonomous agent platform that ingests, standardizes, and unifies veterinary health records for every pet, regardless of clinic, format, or owner. The system works as a persistent background agent for clinics, shelters, and owners—ingesting data via email, SMS, scanned forms, APIs (where available), and even owner-uploaded photos of records. VetRecords AI uses advanced LLMs and graph memory to build a continuously updated "digital health twin" for each pet, mapped to unique identifiers (microchip, tags, owner info). It orchestrates secure, owner-authorized sharing with new vets, boarding, travel, or emergencies via one-tap digital access codes. The human gate ensures owner control and privacy. No more lost vaccine records, duplicate tests, or frantic phone calls—just seamless, autonomous access to accurate pet health data, any time, anywhere.

Architecture

Information flows from three main sources—owners, veterinary clinics, and shelters—into the Ingestion & OCR layer, which standardizes and digitizes any format. Structured and unstructured data is connected by the Graph Memory + LLM Unifier, creating a persistent, comprehensive digital profile for each pet. The Agent Orchestrator monitors for new or changed data, triggers actions (such as sharing records or updating vaccination reminders), and ensures compliance. The Owner Gate puts pet owners in full control, requiring their one-tap approval before data leaves the platform. The Action Layer handles secure record sharing, emergency vet API calls, and travel or boarding data requests on demand.

Build plan (90 days)

Wedge: First 30 days, launch with independent clinics and local shelters—autonomous agents that handle owner-uploaded records and email/fax ingestion. Stack: Next 30 days, integrate with top 5 US veterinary software providers (via RPA/API), and launch direct-to-owner digital record vault app. Pricing: Clinics pay $75/mo for agent record automation, shelters free, owners $2/mo/pet for premium (travel/export/emergency access). Monetize API integrations with boarding, travel, and pet insurance over time.

Why now

Pandemic pet adoption spikes, worker shortages in vet clinics, and growing regulatory push for animal health interoperability make the time right. Every year, more owners expect digital solutions, and AI/LLM advances finally make automated record ingestion and semantic matching viable at scale, even from low-quality scans or text. No one—clinic, owner, or government—wants another lost pet record crisis. VetRecords AI turns a broken, paper-bound mess into seamless, secure, actionable pet health data for everyone who needs it.
