DANDA #085 — ClaimClarity: AI Agents to End Disability Insurance Claim Denial Nightmares for Individuals

AR Akhil Reddy Danda · 14th October, 2026 · 4 min read

Filing a disability insurance claim should be straightforward. But for millions of Americans, the process is a black box—one clogged with paperwork, slow communication, and arbitrary denials. Each denial not only threatens financial security, but also piles on stress to already vulnerable individuals and families. It's time to automate clarity and fairness into this broken system.

The problem

Over 8 million Americans rely on private disability insurance for income when illness or injury keeps them from work. Yet, more than 60% of long-term disability claims are initially denied, and appeals can drag on for 6 months or more—often leaving people without any income. The process is so confusing that over 50% of denied claimants drop out without appealing, even when their claims are valid. Insurers cite "missing documentation" or "policy ambiguity" as reasons for denial, but most rejections result from administrative errors, incomplete forms, or misunderstood medical records. For the average American, fighting for a legitimate disability claim is an exhausting, opaque, and often losing battle.

The idea: ClaimClarity

ClaimClarity is a fully agentic automation platform for individuals filing disability insurance claims. Users connect their insurer portal, medical providers, and employment records. AI agents then:

  • Ingest and organize all relevant policy, employment, and medical information
  • Auto-generate claim packets with precise documentation tailored to each insurer’s requirements
  • Submit claims electronically, tracking every status update and communication
  • Flag missing information or insurer requests instantly, proactively requesting needed documents
  • Maintain a transparent timeline and status for the user, with guided next steps
  • Escalate and prepare appeals automatically if a denial occurs, including generation of supporting arguments and evidence summaries

This flips the script: individuals are no longer at the mercy of arcane insurer requirements or paperwork dead ends. The agent is always working, tracking, and pushing the claim forward—no more "lost in the system." ClaimClarity brings transparency, speed, and confidence to the disability claim process.

Architecture

All user, medical, and insurer inputs are funneled through the Ingestion Layer, which parses and structures unstructured docs and API feeds. The Memory & Graph layer builds a knowledge base of policy rules, timelines, and supporting documents. The Agent Orchestrator runs specialized agents for claim building, submission, status tracking, and appeals. Before any major submission or escalation, the Human Gate presents the AI's proposed action to the user for review and confirmation. The Action Layer executes external communications—submitting claims, requesting records, or auto-filing appeals—while continually syncing status updates.

Build plan (90 days)

  • Wedge: Start with ERISA-governed employer disability plans (covering 50M+ workers), focusing on claims for short-term disability—fewer variables, high pain.
  • Stack: Expand to individual long-term disability, auto-import from major insurer portals, integrate EHR (Epic, Cerner, etc.) for medical evidence gathering, and add proactive appeal drafting.
  • Pricing: Free for filing basic claims; $79/case for full agent-guided navigation including appeals, or $19/mo subscription for unlimited tracking and document automation.

Why now

Disability claims have never been more complex—insurers deploy advanced automation to find denials, but individuals are left in the dark. AI language and workflow agents can now parse complex policy/legal, medical, and employer documentation at scale, and APIs for health/insurance data are finally mature. With millions more Americans in gig or contract roles (and thus less HR support), the need for direct-to-consumer, always-on claim navigation is urgent. ClaimClarity is the missing automation layer to rebalance power and end the disability claim denial nightmare for ordinary people.
