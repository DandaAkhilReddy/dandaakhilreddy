DANDA #123 — RecallShield: AI Agents to End Medical Device Recall Notification Failures for Patients

AR Akhil Reddy Danda · 21st November, 2026 · 4 min read

Every year, thousands of medical devices—pacemakers, joint replacements, insulin pumps—are recalled due to serious safety defects. But most patients never find out. Hospitals and clinics still rely on outdated, manual processes to match recall lists to patient records. The result is a recurring nightmare: patients with dangerous devices remain unaware, and preventable injuries or deaths occur for months or years after a recall. It’s a hidden crisis at the heart of healthcare safety.

The problem

In the US alone, over 310 million medical devices are implanted or used every year. The FDA issues an average of 1,200 device recalls annually, but up to 80% of impacted patients are never directly notified. Hospitals struggle to trace device serial numbers in fragmented EHRs, and manual contact efforts miss patients, especially those who move or change providers. The result: in one high-profile case, 170,000 patients were left unaware of a pacemaker recall for over a year. Lawsuits, avoidable injuries, and trust erosion have cost the system over $1.6B annually in litigation and remediation. This is a problem that spans every hospital network, device manufacturer, and patient in the country.

The idea: RecallShield

RecallShield is an agentic automation platform that acts as a "recall concierge" for healthcare networks. It directly integrates with hospital EHRs, national device registries, and FDA recall feeds. When a recall is issued, RecallShield’s AI agents instantly map affected device serials to patient records, verify contact details, and orchestrate personalized notifications to patients, caregivers, and their physicians—by text, email, or phone, with tracked acknowledgment. Agents also update care plans, flag at-risk patients in the EHR, and provide guided next steps. No more missed recalls or weeks of manual matching. For regulators and device makers, RecallShield delivers real-time compliance dashboards and audit trails, closing the loop from recall to resolution in days—not months.

Architecture

RecallShield ingests FDA recall feeds, national device registry data, and EHRs. The ingestion engine maps devices to patients, builds a knowledge graph linking patients, devices, and recalls, and triggers RecallAI agents. The Agent Orchestrator matches recalls to individual patients, drafts notifications, and routes cases flagged for human review (e.g., complex cases, ambiguity) to care teams via the Human Gate. Once approved, the Action Layer delivers personalized notifications to patients (SMS, email, portal), updates EHRs, and logs all actions for audit and compliance. The process is end-to-end automated, with human oversight where required for clinical or ethical judgment.

Build plan (90 days)

Wedge: Target mid-size hospital systems with high implant volumes (joint replacements, cardiac devices). Integrate with top 2 EHRs (Epic, Cerner) and FDA recall API. Stack: Layer device registry connectors (UDI, GUDID), notification modules (Twilio/email), and dashboarding. Pricing: Per-patient annual license ($1–3/patient/year), plus compliance/audit module upsells for larger health systems. Expand to include device manufacturers for direct recall compliance and analytics.

Why now

The regulatory landscape is shifting: hospitals are facing new requirements to prove recall notification compliance, while device recalls are on the rise and interoperability standards (FHIR, UDI) finally make cross-system matching feasible. Patient safety is a board-level issue, and recent litigation has turned C-suite attention to this "last mile" liability. AI agentic automation can finally close the recall gap—at the speed and scale modern healthcare demands.
