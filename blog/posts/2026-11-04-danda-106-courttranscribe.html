DANDA #106 — CourtTranscribe: AI Agents to End Courtroom Transcript Delays and Errors

AR Akhil Reddy Danda · 4th November, 2026 · 3 min read

Every day, courtrooms across the U.S. generate hours of spoken testimony, arguments, and rulings. But the official transcript—the backbone of appeals, records, and justice—often doesn’t arrive for weeks or months. Lawyers, judges, and litigants are stuck waiting, and the process is riddled with costly errors and bottlenecks. I know first-hand how a single transcription mistake can alter the outcome of a case.

The problem

There are over 30,000 court reporters in the U.S., but demand far outpaces supply. In California alone, average transcript wait times are 3–8 weeks after proceedings, with transcription errors costing litigants and courts millions annually. Nationally, courts pay over $400 million a year for transcription, with 20%+ of transcripts containing material errors requiring correction or appeals. Meanwhile, 40% of rural courts report severe delays due to chronic court reporter shortages. In criminal appeals, delayed or missing transcripts can lead to overturned verdicts or retrials. With a backlog of over 400,000 untranscribed hours per year, the system is buckling.

The idea: CourtTranscribe

Let’s fix this. CourtTranscribe is an agent-powered AI platform that ingests courtroom audio/video in real time, generates certified transcripts within hours, flags ambiguous sections for human review, and delivers error-free records directly to judges, attorneys, and clerks. The AI agent orchestrates ingestion, diarization, legal formatting, and compliance checks—integrating with court record systems and providing explainable audit trails. No more waiting weeks. No more missed deadlines.

Architecture

Here’s how it flows: courtroom audio/video feeds enter via Inputs; the Ingestion module syncs and processes streams in realtime. Memory/Graph layer separates speakers (judge, attorneys, witnesses) and applies legal context. The Agent Orchestrator runs transcription, applies legal formatting, and checks compliance. Ambiguous or low-confidence sections are routed through the Human Gate for review. Final transcripts are delivered and logged in the Action Layer, with full audit trails and integrations to court record systems.

Build plan (90 days)

Our wedge: county-level courts in rural areas facing the highest delays. Stack: ingest and diarize courtroom audio (week 1–3), auto-transcribe and legal-format (week 4–6), human review workflow (week 7–8), transcript delivery/audit integration (week 9–12). Pricing: $49/case base, plus $0.15/minute audio, with volume discounts for courts and law firms. By day 90, we’ll have pilots with two counties, and integrations with major law practice platforms.

Why now

Speech-to-text tech is reliable at legal-grade, and public sector adoption is surging. Courts face budget and staffing crises, and attorneys demand faster, error-free transcripts. The market is ripe for disruption—AI agents can deliver not just speed, but traceable accuracy and compliance for a critical justice function. CourtTranscribe will unlock justice, efficiency, and savings—where it matters most.
