DANDA #040 — TicketWise: AI Agents to End Live Event Ticketing Chaos and Flip the Scalper Game

AR Akhil Reddy Danda · 30th August, 2026 · 3 min read

Nothing kills the magic of a live event faster than ticketing chaos. Whether it's a concert, sports game, or theater show, fans are forced to battle bots, scalpers, and outdated systems that can't handle real demand. The result: missed moments, wasted money, and venues hemorrhaging revenue.

The problem

Every year, over 900 million tickets are sold for live events in the US. But the system is stacked against real fans: over 39% report failing to get tickets due to bot activity, and venues lose an estimated $2.6 billion annually to fraud and inefficient distribution. Scalpers scoop up as much as 60% of top-tier tickets within seconds—directly impacting affordability and fairness. Legit resale is slow or blocked, and more than 15% of online tickets are flagged as suspicious, leading to legal headaches and customer rage. Event organizers spend days manually resolving allocation disputes and refund nightmares. The broken ticketing pipeline is not just a nuisance—it's a billion-dollar bleed-out for both venues and artists.

The idea: TicketWise

TicketWise is a turnkey AI-agent platform for venues and event organizers. It autonomously manages live ticket allocation, detects bots and scalper networks in real time, and handles instant resale and refunds with agentic automation. Each agent acts as a gatekeeper, ensuring fair distribution, resolving disputes (e.g., duplicate purchases, last-minute cancellations), and flagging suspicious activity before it reaches fans. TicketWise plugs into existing ticketing systems with API adapters, deploys graph-based bot detection, and creates agent-driven workflows for resale and dispute resolution—all without human bottlenecks.

Architecture

The flow starts with ticketing APIs feeding live session and purchase data to the Ingestion layer. Graph DB rapidly profiles bot and fraud activity using real-time network detection. AgentCore orchestrates ticket allocation, resale, and dispute workflows, looping in Venue Ops for manual overrides where needed. The Action Layer then triggers ticket releases, refunds, or blocks—closing the loop instantly and fairly.

Build plan (90 days)

Wedge: Launch with mid-size concert venues (500-5,000 seats) suffering most from bot scalping and slow resale. Stack: API adapters for popular ticketing systems (Eventbrite, Ticketmaster), Python agent core, Neo4j graph DB for fraud profiling, secure Stripe/ACH integrations for instant refunds and payments, web dashboard for venue ops. Pricing: SaaS, $499/month per venue (up to 10,000 tickets/month), plus 1% of resale/refund transactions. First 10 venues get onboarding free.

Why now

Bot and scalper sophistication exploded post-pandemic—and venues can't keep up. Fans are fed up, regulators are circling, and recent advances in real-time graph-based fraud detection make agentic automation viable at scale. TicketWise is the AI wedge to flip the ticketing game from chaos to fairness—putting venues and fans in control, and scalpers on notice.
