DANDA #128 — ScanWise AI: Autonomous Agents to End Radiology Backlogs and Diagnostic Errors

AR Akhil Reddy Danda · 26th November, 2026 · 4 min read

Every year, millions of Americans wait days or even weeks for radiology results, with delays leading to missed or late diagnoses that worsen patient outcomes. Radiology departments are drowning in a tsunami of CTs, MRIs, and X-rays, with too few specialists and surging scan volumes. The result? Real people fall through the cracks—and some never recover.

The problem

Across the U.S., over 1 billion imaging exams are performed annually. Radiologist shortages are acute—nearly 50% of U.S. counties have zero local radiologist. Report turnarounds often exceed 24-48 hours in busy hospitals, and in some cases, even non-urgent scans wait weeks. In the U.K., the backlog for reporting scans exceeded 626,000 patients in 2023. Diagnostic errors are even more troubling—missed findings in imaging contribute to an estimated 40,000-80,000 deaths per year in the U.S. alone. This backlog has human costs: late-stage cancer diagnoses, missed strokes, and unnecessary suffering.

The idea: ScanWise AI

ScanWise AI is an agentic automation platform for radiology teams. It deploys specialized autonomous agents to:

  • Continuously ingest all new imaging scans (CT, MRI, X-ray, ultrasound) from PACS/RIS systems in real-time
  • Auto-triage studies for urgency using AI models (e.g., flag suspected bleeds, masses, fractures, pneumothorax, etc.)
  • Surface high-risk cases for immediate review by a radiologist, reducing critical delays
  • Flag potentially missed findings in finalized reports, using AI to cross-check scan images against dictated text
  • Orchestrate follow-ups: directly notify referring clinicians and coordinate with human staff for patient outreach
  • Provide analytics dashboards to hospital admins on backlog, turnaround, and error rates

This is not just "AI reading images"—it’s an agentic workflow engine that eliminates bottlenecks and catches what humans miss, all while keeping radiologists in the loop and in control.

Architecture

ScanWise AI plugs directly into hospital imaging (PACS/RIS) and EHR systems. As new scans come in, they're ingested, parsed, and stored in a case graph. The Agent Orchestrator analyzes each scan, triages for urgency, flags possible findings, and routes high-risk cases to the human radiologist gate. Cleared actions—such as updating the EHR, notifying clinicians, or surfacing analytics—are then executed by the Action Layer. Throughout, every step is audited and feedback loops improve the agents' accuracy over time.

Build plan (90 days)

  • Wedge: Sell to mid-sized hospital systems overwhelmed by CT/X-ray backlogs—start with AI-powered triage for acute findings (e.g., intracranial bleeds, pneumothorax)
  • Stack: Expand to cover MRI, mammography, ultrasound. Layer in agent-driven analytics, missed finding detection, and automated clinician notification. Build integrations for Epic, Cerner, and leading PACS/RIS providers
  • Pricing: SaaS, $5-15 per case analyzed (volume-based), or $10k/mo/site for unlimited with SLAs, audit, and analytics dashboards. Enterprise compliance and support add-ons

Why now

Radiology scan volumes are exploding, but the number of radiologists is flatlining. Recent advances in multimodal foundation models and agentic automation mean AI can now reliably flag life-threatening findings and automate workflow, not just "read images." Hospitals are under regulatory and reputational pressure to reduce diagnostic errors and backlogs. With reimbursement models moving to value-based care, eliminating delays and errors isn't just good medicine—it's good business. ScanWise AI unlocks a step-change in how radiology teams operate, saving lives and reducing burnout, right when the system needs it most.
