DANDA #093 — CaseReady: AI Agents to End Court Evidence Submission Chaos

AR Akhil Reddy Danda · 22nd October, 2026 · 4 min read

Every day, thousands of court cases in the U.S. are delayed, derailed, or even dismissed due to issues with evidence submission—lost files, missing chain-of-custody documentation, mislabeling, and compliance errors. For law firms, prosecutors, and courts, the workflow of managing evidence is a high-stakes, high-friction ordeal that wastes time, drives up costs, and slows the wheels of justice.

The problem

The evidence submission process is chaos. U.S. courts handled over 18 million criminal and civil cases last year. Yet, more than 36% of attorneys report cases delayed or dismissed due to evidence management issues—missing documents, improperly authenticated files, and broken chain-of-custody records. Courts and law firms spend $8B+ annually on manual evidence handling, audits, and compliance. Worse: digital evidence (from CCTV, phones, emails, social media, IoT) is exploding in volume, yet most courts lack automated tools to verify, audit, and track it. The result: justice delayed (months wasted), justice denied (cases lost), and costs that devastate smaller firms and public defenders.

The idea: CaseReady

CaseReady is an autonomous AI agent platform that ingests, verifies, organizes, and tracks all types of evidence—physical and digital—from the moment it's logged until it is presented in court. It automates chain-of-custody, detects errors/omissions, proactively checks compliance with local rules, and creates auditable, judge-ready evidence packets. CaseReady integrates with court and law firm systems, emails, and drives, dramatically reducing delays, human errors, and evidence-loss risk.

Architecture

Evidence files from uploads, scanned docs, emails, and court APIs are ingested and parsed (with OCR/metadata tagging), then mapped to an auditable case graph database. The agent orchestrator verifies chain-of-custody, audits compliance, and checks for missing or erroneous data. Human legal staff review/approve before CaseReady submits evidence packets and logs actions—ensuring readiness for court and full traceability.

Build plan (90 days)

Wedge: Target midsize law firms and public defender offices handling high volumes of digital evidence in state criminal courts. Integrate with Clio and MyCase for instant value.
Stack: Add support for civil litigation, DA offices, and direct court clerk portals. Roll out advanced AI-powered video/audio evidence parsing and automated compliance updates as new local rules emerge.
Pricing: Per-case SaaS ($99/case/mo), with firm/court volume tiers and audit/proof-of-chain reporting as premium upsells.

Why now

Evidence volume is exploding—bodycams, digital docs, IoT, and social media are now standard parts of most cases. Courts and small firms are drowning in manual handling and compliance risk. Judges are demanding better audit trails and faster submissions. Recent advances in LLMs, document AI, and chain-of-custody digital signatures make full automation feasible and court-credible for the first time. AI is the only way to scale justice with accuracy, transparency, and speed.
