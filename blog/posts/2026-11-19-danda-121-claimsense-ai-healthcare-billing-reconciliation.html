DANDA #121 — ClaimSense AI: End Medical Bill vs. Insurance EOB Headaches for Millions

AR Akhil Reddy Danda · 19th November, 2026 · 3 min read

Every year, tens of millions of Americans get medical bills they don’t understand. Hospital statements, insurance explanations of benefits (EOBs), and real out-of-pocket costs rarely match. Patients and billing staff spend hours (or weeks) playing phone tag and hunting for what’s actually owed. This chaos creates stress, surprise medical debt, and wasted administrative cost—and it’s only getting worse as healthcare pricing grows more complex.

The problem

The US medical billing system is chaos by design. Over 100 million Americans struggle with medical debt, often due to billing confusion or errors. A 2022 survey showed 41% of adults have medical or dental bills they’re paying off, and 66% of bankruptcies cite medical bills as a factor. Hospitals, meanwhile, spend $39 billion a year on billing and insurance-related admin—much of it just reconciling claims, EOBs, and payments. Patients receive bills that don’t match EOBs, get hit with surprise balances, or overpay because they can’t decipher the paperwork. Billing departments waste days on the phone with insurers and confused patients, slowing cashflow and tanking patient satisfaction.

The idea: ClaimSense AI

ClaimSense AI is an agentic automation platform that ingests patient medical bills and insurance EOBs (scanned, PDF, or portal scraped), instantly reconciles every line item, and delivers a plain-English breakdown of what’s owed and why. The AI flags mismatches, overbilling, and insurer errors, auto-generates patient-friendly explanations, and even drafts correction requests for billing teams or appeals for patients. For providers, it cuts phone calls and back-office gruntwork; for patients, it unlocks clarity and prevents unnecessary debt. The platform works with any US insurer or hospital billing format, using LLMs and specialized logic to map CPT/ICD codes, modifiers, covered amounts, denials, and patient responsibility in seconds—then orchestrates correction or appeal workflows as needed.

Architecture

The flow starts with patient or billing staff uploading or connecting bills and EOBs (Inputs). The Ingestion Layer uses OCR and custom parsers to extract and map data, routing it to the Claims Graph in Memory & Graph for normalization and relationship mapping (codes, amounts, rules). The Agent Orchestrator—powered by LLMs and a compliance rules engine—reconciles bills with EOBs, flags mismatches, and generates explanations. The Human Gate surfaces potential issues to a billing staffer or patient for review, then the Action Layer outputs clear breakdowns, suggested corrections, and draft appeals or correction letters—all tailored to the specific insurer and provider rules.

Build plan (90 days)

Wedge: Pilot with ambulatory clinics and specialty practices (who have high patient billing confusion and phone burden). First stack: Web portal + secure upload, agentic reconciliation, EOB/bill mapping, explanation PDF, mismatch flagging, and sample patient appeal/correction letter generation. Early pricing: $200/mo per provider location (unlimited bills/EOBs), or $9.99 direct-to-consumer per session. Stack expands to bulk ingestion, direct billing/EHR/EOB API integrations, white-label for RCM vendors, and advanced analytics dashboards.

Why now

Healthcare billing complexity and patient out-of-pocket risk are both rising fast, with record levels of medical debt and regulatory scrutiny (e.g., No Surprises Act). LLMs now handle complex coding, context, and document reconciliation—making instant, agentic explanation and correction finally viable. Providers are desperate to cut billing friction, patients demand transparency, and payers must reduce complaints and regulatory risk. There’s never been a sharper wedge to fix this root cause of US medical financial pain.
