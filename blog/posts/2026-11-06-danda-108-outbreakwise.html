DANDA #108 — OutbreakWise: Autonomous AI Agents to Slash School Illness Outbreaks Before They Spread

AR Akhil Reddy Danda · 6th November, 2026 · 4 min read

Every year, illness outbreaks in K-12 schools lead to millions of lost class days, anxious parents, and staff shortages—often because warning signs are missed or interventions come too late. Yet the daily signals for trouble (nurse logs, absences, symptom reports) already exist in digital silos. I know firsthand: outbreak tracking is a manual, paper-and-email mess—costing kids, teachers, and districts dearly.

The problem

School outbreaks of flu, norovirus, RSV, and strep cost the US over 22 million lost school days per year. Absentee rates can spike by 40-60% during outbreaks, and districts face substitute teacher shortages and even forced closures. Yet over 70% of schools still rely on manual spreadsheets or phone trees for illness tracking. Often, reporting to local health officials is delayed by days, and targeted cleaning or parent alerts come too late. The result: preventable spread, learning loss, and avoidable public health cost. In 2024, the CDC reported over 11,000 school-associated outbreaks—but the real number is higher, since most mild outbreaks are never formally tracked or acted on. No one should be flying blind when kid health is at stake.

The idea: OutbreakWise

OutbreakWise is an autonomous agentic platform that monitors live signals from school nurse logs, absence systems, and parent symptom reports. It detects early outbreak clusters using graph-based pattern recognition, triages severity, and orchestrates rapid, stepwise interventions: internal alerts, parent communications, targeted cleaning requests, and real-time reporting to local health authorities. Crucially, it slots into existing student information systems and works with minimal setup. OutbreakWise doesn’t just surface trends—it acts to coordinate the right human and automated responses before chaos hits.

Architecture

The workflow: Inputs arrive from nurse logs, absence data, and parent self-reports. The ingestion layer parses, normalizes, and runs NLP-trigger detection. The Memory/Graph layer builds a real-time illness graph by classroom, grade, and school. The Agent Orchestrator continuously scans for spatial/temporal clusters, triages severity, and decides on next best actions. Human Gate lets a nurse/admin review before broadcast actions. The Action Layer automates alerts, cleaning dispatch, health reporting, and targeted parent notifications—closing the loop in hours, not days. Signals and status updates are fed back into the graph for ongoing learning.

Build plan (90 days)

Wedge: Launch with 2-3 midsize, tech-forward school districts (~10,000 students) for pilot. Integrate with popular SIS (e.g., PowerSchool), nurse log exports, and Google Forms for parent input. Train outbreak detection on local historical patterns.
Stack: Layer in staff/parent notification, cleaning dispatch integration (email/SMS/webhook), and one-click health authority reporting. Add district dashboard for real-time outbreak mapping.
Pricing: SaaS per-school subscription, starting at $170/month per school (covers up to 1,000 students), plus optional district analytics/alerting tier. Flat pilot pricing for first 90 days, then annual contracts.

Why now

Post-pandemic, districts are under pressure to reduce lost learning days and improve public health responsiveness. Schools are embracing cloud SIS, digital nurse logs, and real-time messaging—making agentic automation feasible for the first time. AI-powered outbreak detection can work in weeks, not months. OutbreakWise empowers schools to act fast, keep kids healthy, and stay ahead of outbreaks—before they shut doors again.
