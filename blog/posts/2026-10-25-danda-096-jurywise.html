DANDA #096 — JuryWise: AI Agents to End Jury Duty Selection Chaos and No-Show Epidemics

AR Akhil Reddy Danda · 25th October, 2026 · 4 min read

Every year, millions of Americans dread the arrival of a jury duty summons. But for courts, the real nightmare isn't apathy—it's chaos. From undelivered mail to confusing eligibility checks, last-minute deferrals, and sky-high no-show rates, jury selection is a logistical mess. The result: wasted time, millions squandered, and a civic process undermined by inefficiency.

The problem

US courts summon over 32 million people for jury duty annually, but nearly 20% never respond or show up. Major metro areas see no-show rates as high as 30%, forcing courts to oversummon and waste resources. Cancellations, address errors, and manual eligibility checks generate hundreds of thousands of staff hours. In Los Angeles County alone, jury management costs top $14M per year, with over 100,000 potential jurors failing to respond annually. Even when people want to serve, confusing paperwork or inaccessible processes drive away qualified citizens, disproportionately harming low-income communities. The result: delays, failed trials, and a public perception of jury duty as broken and punitive.

The idea: JuryWise

JuryWise is an AI-powered agentic automation platform for courts that transforms the entire jury selection and management process. Instead of relying on static mailers and overburdened clerks, JuryWise deploys autonomous agents to:

  • Track down and verify addresses using real-time data
  • Personalize eligibility checks and automate digital forms
  • Send reminders and explanations via preferred channels (SMS, email, WhatsApp, TTS phone)
  • Handle deferral, hardship, and rescheduling requests conversationally—eliminating paperwork
  • Give courts live dashboards on attendance risk and optimize future juror pools

The result: Less manual labor, higher legitimacy, and drastically fewer no-shows—restoring dignity and efficiency to a core civic duty.

Architecture

How it works: Court datasets and juror-sourced updates flow into Data Ingestion, which resolves identity, address, and eligibility. The Juror Graph DB builds a real-time history and status for each juror. The Agent Orchestrator manages personalized, language-adaptive outreach, reminders, and dynamic eligibility handling. Edge cases or appeals route to the Human Gate, a staff dashboard for manual review or override. Finally, the Action Layer automates notices (SMS, email, IVR) and produces actionable dashboards for court managers.

Build plan (90 days)

  • Wedge: Launch as a plug-in for existing jury management systems in 1 midsize county (100k annual summonses). Focus on automating eligibility checks, digital comms, and rescheduling workflows—eliminating 50% of staff manual labor in 30 days.
  • Stack: Add language adaptation, TTS phone calls, and real-time analytics for comms effectiveness. Expand to adjacent counties by providing API hooks for state-level juror pool management.
  • Pricing: Per-summon SaaS model: $2-3 per managed juror (5–10x ROI per avoided no-show or staff hour saved); volume/municipal contracts for full-county or statewide deployments.

Why now

The status quo is unsustainable: ballooning court costs, public frustration, and digital-native jurors expecting better. With AI communication, instant data validation, and agentic automation, it’s finally possible to transform jury management from adversarial to efficient. Trust in civic duty depends on it—and every dollar saved is a court case advanced.
