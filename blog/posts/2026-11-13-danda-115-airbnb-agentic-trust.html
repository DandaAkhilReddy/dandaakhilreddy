DANDA #115 — TrustGuard: AI Agents to End Short-Term Rental Host/Guest Fraud and Booking Nightmares

AR Akhil Reddy Danda · 13th November, 2026 · 4 min read

Every year, tens of millions of travelers and hosts turn to short-term rental platforms like Airbnb and VRBO. But with 7+ million active listings, rapid growth has fueled a surge of fraud, fake listings, and booking chaos. Hosts lose revenue, guests lose trust, and platforms face mounting disputes—costing the industry billions.

The problem

Short-term rental fraud is real and growing fast. In 2025, North America alone saw over 10,000 reported fake Airbnb listings, resulting in an estimated $150M in lost guest funds. Host identity hijacking and double-booking scams are rampant: 31% of Airbnb hosts have experienced booking disputes, and guest complaints about misleading listings are up 21% year-over-year. Platforms struggle to vet listings and resolve disputes efficiently, burning time and trust—while 1 in 5 guests worry about fraud before every booking. The global short-term rental market, valued at $115B+ in 2024, is losing at least $500M annually to these breakdowns. Existing systems rely heavily on manual review, slow support, and fragmented signals—failing to keep pace with scale and sophistication of scams.

The idea: TrustGuard

TrustGuard delivers autonomous AI agents that plug directly into platforms like Airbnb, VRBO, or even independent hosts' booking systems. The agents pre-screen listings, validate host identities, scan for fraud signals, and intercept suspicious bookings before money changes hands. When disputes arise, TrustGuard orchestrates evidence gathering and real-time negotiation—slashing resolution time from days to minutes. It leverages live data, behavioral signals, and knowledge graphs to flag risks and surface actionable recommendations to human moderators only when truly needed. For guests, TrustGuard offers an instant pre-booking fraud check, and for hosts, it provides automatic dispute defense and resolution tools. The result: fewer scams, faster booking, and restored trust—all agentically automated.

Architecture

Data flows from rental platform APIs (Inputs) through real-time ingestion, mapping to a fraud signal graph (Memory). The Agent Orchestrator analyzes bookings, hosts, and guests, triggering autonomous actions—alerts, dispute resolution, or transaction holds. Human moderators intervene only at the Human Gate for ambiguous cases. The Action Layer pushes real-time updates to Host and Guest Portals for transparent, instant trust-building. The system is designed for plug-and-play integration with any rental platform, scaling from individual hosts to enterprise.

Build plan (90 days)

Wedge: Start with independent hosts and boutique rental managers, offering TrustGuard as an API/portal for fraud screening and dispute automation.
Stack: Expand to plug-ins for Airbnb, VRBO, and property management software; integrate with payment processors and identity verification services.
Pricing: Flat $15/mo per host, or $0.50 per booking screened; tiered enterprise pricing for platforms (starting $2,500/mo).

Why now

Short-term rental growth is outpacing platform trust and support. Fraud is rising, and hosts/guests demand rapid, reliable validation and dispute resolution. AI agentic automation is ready to tackle dynamic signals and scale—restoring confidence, cutting costly losses, and giving platforms a competitive edge. With billions at stake and bookings rising, TrustGuard’s timing is perfect.
