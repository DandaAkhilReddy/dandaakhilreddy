DANDA #104 — RxGuard: AI Agents to End Pharmacy Prior Authorization Fax Nightmares

AR Akhil Reddy Danda · 2nd November, 2026 · 5 min read

Every day, millions of patients in the U.S. walk away from pharmacies empty-handed, not because of drug shortages or payment issues, but because their prescription is stuck in 'prior authorization' hell. Pharmacies, physicians, and insurers grind through a mountain of faxed forms, phone calls, and broken workflows. This isn’t a niche pain point—it delays care for tens of millions and contributes directly to prescription abandonment and poor health outcomes.

The problem

Prior authorization (PA) is the process where insurers require extra paperwork before they’ll pay for many prescriptions. It’s supposed to control costs, but it’s a human and operational disaster. Over 200 million prior authorization requests are made every year in the U.S. 94% of doctors report care delays due to PA, and 80% say patients abandon treatment altogether due to slow approval. The average PA request takes 3-7 days to resolve, involving faxes, phone tag, and endless form-filling. Pharmacies spend 20+ hours per week per location chasing paperwork, while practices lose $41K per physician annually in wasted time. Worst of all, up to 37% of patients simply give up and never pick up needed medication when PA is required. That’s not a workflow hiccup—it’s a nationwide care gap for diabetes, cancer, mental health, and more.

The idea: RxGuard

RxGuard is the AI agent that ends prior authorization nightmares for pharmacies, clinics, and patients—no more fax machines, no more phone tag, no more abandoned care. RxGuard connects directly (via secure APIs, OCR, and smart document capture) to pharmacy management systems, EMRs, and insurer portals. When a PA is triggered for a prescription, RxGuard auto-ingests the request, extracts key data with LLMs, and compiles the relevant clinical and insurance details in real time. The agent orchestrates end-to-end communication: pre-filling PA forms, routing to the right insurer, checking requirements, following up automatically, flagging exceptions to humans, and alerting patients when their meds are ready. Where possible, the agent submits via ePA (electronic prior auth) or intelligently faxes (with digital tracking) only as a last resort. The result: pharmacies and clinics cut PA time from days to minutes, and patients finally get their meds—no more falling through the cracks.

Architecture

Pharmacy management, EMR/EHR, and payer portals feed raw prescription and coverage signals into RxGuard’s ingestion engine. The platform uses LLMs and OCR to extract key data. Patient, provider, and pharmacy relationships are mapped in a secure graph. The agent orchestrator dynamically compiles and submits the PA—routing via ePA or smart fax as needed—while exceptions or missing info are routed to human review. Action endpoints trigger instant ePA, fax fallback, or direct notification to providers/patients when the PA is approved, reducing cycle time from days to minutes.

Build plan (90 days)

Wedge: Focus on high-volume independent and small-chain pharmacies in states with the worst PA delays (e.g., Texas, Florida). Integrate with top pharmacy management software; offer plug-and-play agent onboarding. Stack: Add integrations for regional EMRs (for clinics) and major payers. Expand agent capabilities for advanced form comprehension, fax-to-digital conversion, and real patient/provider notification workflows. Pricing: SaaS per-location: $499/mo base, plus $1.10 per successful PA (or cost-per-minute saved for large chains). Free for patients, opt-in alerts and tracking. Future: enterprise health systems, large chains, health IT partners.

Why now

Payer, provider, and pharmacy tech stacks have finally opened APIs and standardized ePA schemas. The staffing crisis in pharmacies and clinics makes manual PAs unsustainable. Recent regulatory pressure threatens fines for delayed or mishandled PAs, while patients are more willing than ever to switch pharmacies based on fulfillment speed. LLMs and agentic automation now handle the messy, semi-structured world of faxes and forms with human-level accuracy. The era of PA-as-obstacle is finally ending—RxGuard makes sure it ends for good.
