Microsoft Dataverse Gets Embedded Databricks, Smashing Data Silos for App Devs

AR Akhil Reddy Danda · 27th September, 2026 · 2 min read

Microsoft is quietly killing one of the most annoying headaches in enterprise app development: data silos between business apps and analytics. This week, Dataverse — the data backbone for Power Apps, Copilot Studio, and Dynamics — now lets you spin up a Databricks Lakehouse workspace directly inside the Dataverse UI. No more brittle connectors, no more copy-paste hacks. Native. Live. Unified.

Why It Matters

If you’ve ever built an app on Dataverse or Power Platform, you know the pain. Business data lives in Dataverse tables, but anything serious — like building ML models or running real-time analytics — means clumsy ETL to Azure Data Lake or Spark. The modes just… didn’t talk to each other well. Now, you get Databricks-native Spark clusters as first-class citizens, with access control, streaming, and data versioning managed in one place.

For Engineers

This isn’t just about convenience. Now you can train ML models directly on live business data, version notebooks alongside app code, and push results back to Power Apps with zero data movement. Governance is unified too; RBAC, lineage, and audit are all handled at the Dataverse level. The net effect: faster iteration, way less risk of sync bugs, and more chances to blend ‘citizen dev’ with genuine engineering muscle.

Bottom line: If you’re building anything in the Microsoft stack, you now have a real data platform — not just a toy database for business workflows. That’s a game-changer for any org trying to ship AI-infused apps at speed.
